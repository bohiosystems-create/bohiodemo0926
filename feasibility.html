Project details

Land acquisition

Purchase price is paid as a deposit at the deposit month and the balance at settlement. Transfer tax, legal and brokerage are entered as cost lines on the Costs tab using the “% of land” basis.

Performance targets & escalation

Sales revenue

Sale price per unit = area × rate. Settlements are spread from the start month over the span. Revenue escalation (Project tab) is applied from month 0.

Leased / investment revenue

Net rent is received from the lease start month until the sale month, when the asset is sold at net rent ÷ capitalisation rate, less purchaser’s costs.

Revenue summary

Development costs

Basis options: Lump sum; Qty × rate; % of construction (all lump-sum and rate lines in the Construction category); % of land; % of GRV, spread over the timing you set; % of revenue, paid as revenue comes in, for sales commission. S-curve uses a smooth bell-shaped spend profile.

Funding structure

Costs are funded in the order equity → mezzanine → senior debt. Interest is calculated monthly on the opening balance and capitalised. Revenue repays senior debt first, then mezzanine, then returns equity and profit. The senior limit is set as a share of total development cost, including finance, and is solved iteratively. Any shortfall beyond all facilities is flagged and funded as extra equity.
